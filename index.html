Thread to Self

One thread. You and your future self.

A private, continuous message stream on your iPhone. Capture thoughts. Move on.

How Thread to Self works

Everything you write joins one continuous thread. No folders, no sorting, nothing to organize.

Once a message is added, it can't be edited - like something you actually said. You can only delete it forever.

Your words stay on your iPhone and your private iCloud. Nowhere else.

Deliberately left out

Thread to Self is defined as much by what it doesn't do:

  • Photos and attachments
  • Tags and folders
  • Search and filters
  • Editing old messages
  • AI features
  • Social features
  • Ads
